AFFILIATE TERMS

Effective upon signup  ·  Last updated 2025

The short version: you share your link, someone signs up and becomes a paying client through it, we pay you $50. These terms spell out exactly how that works, what counts as a qualifying signup, and what isn't allowed.

01 — Who can participate

Anyone 18 or older with an audience or network they're willing to share their link with. You don't need a minimum follower count or a specific platform. You do need to be an actual person — no bots, no automated traffic, no link farms.

02 — What counts as a qualifying signup

A qualifying signup is a new client who:

  • Arrives at joinironhouse.com via your unique referral link
  • Completes the matching quiz and is paired with a trainer
  • Starts a paid coaching engagement

Signups that don't convert to a paid engagement do not qualify for a commission. Referral attribution is tracked via your unique link — the client must use it at the time of their visit for the referral to register.

03 — Commission rate

$50 flat per qualifying signup. There is no cap on how many you can earn. Rates may be updated for future signups with 30 days' notice — any signups that occurred before a rate change are paid at the rate in effect at the time of that signup.

04 — Payouts

Payouts are processed monthly. After your first qualifying signup, we'll email you to collect your preferred payment method (PayPal or Venmo). The minimum payout threshold is $50 — one qualifying signup. Earned commissions are always paid out, including upon termination of the relationship.

05 — What's not allowed

The following will disqualify signups and may result in removal from the program:

  • Self-referrals — you cannot sign yourself up through your own link
  • Paid advertising using your referral link without prior written approval from Iron House
  • Spam or misleading claims — misrepresenting what Iron House is, what it costs, or what the affiliate program pays
  • Attribution manipulation — cookie stuffing, forced clicks, or any method designed to generate fraudulent referral credit

06 — Disclosure

You are required to clearly disclose that you earn a commission when sharing your affiliate link. A simple statement like "I may earn a commission if you sign up through my link" is sufficient. This is both an FTC requirement and the right thing to do for your audience. Iron House is not responsible for non-disclosure by affiliates.

07 — Iron House's rights

Iron House reserves the right to verify the legitimacy of any referred signup before issuing payment. We may withhold or reverse a commission if we determine the signup was fraudulent, self-referred, or obtained through methods that violate these terms.

08 — Termination

Either party can end the affiliate relationship at any time, for any reason, with no notice required. If Iron House terminates your participation due to a violation of these terms, commissions tied to unconverted signups (clicks or quiz completions that haven't yet become paid engagements) are forfeited. Commissions already earned on confirmed paid engagements will still be paid out on the next monthly cycle.

09 — Changes to these terms

Iron House may update these terms at any time. For material changes, we will notify active affiliates by email at least 30 days before the changes take effect. Continuing to use your referral link after that date constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

10 — Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Florida. Any disputes arising under or related to this agreement will be handled in Hillsborough County, Florida.
