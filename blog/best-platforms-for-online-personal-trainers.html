The best platforms for online personal trainers in 2025

By Iron House · 6 min read

Most platforms were built to fill a marketplace. Iron House was built to make a match. The difference matters more than you'd think.

Why platform choice matters more than most trainers realize

The platform you choose shapes the kind of clients you get, the quality of those relationships, and ultimately whether your online coaching business grows or stalls. Yet most trainers pick a platform the same way they choose a gym — they go with the first one they hear about.

Here's a more honest look at what's out there.

What to look for in a personal trainer platform

Lead quality over lead volume

A platform that sends you 20 weak leads is worse than one that sends you 3 strong ones. Pre-qualification matters. Does the platform match clients to trainers based on compatibility, or does it just show a directory and hope for the best?

Fee structure

Most platforms charge monthly subscriptions regardless of whether you get clients. Others take a commission on earnings. Commission-only models align the platform's incentives with yours — they only earn when you earn.

How clients discover you

Directory-style platforms require clients to scroll and compare. Matching-based platforms curate a shortlist for each client. The latter produces dramatically higher conversion rates because the client arrives already interested.

Ownership of the client relationship

Some platforms insert themselves between you and your client permanently. Others connect you and step back. The best platforms connect you, take a fair cut, and get out of the way.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be on multiple platforms?

Not necessarily. Quality beats quantity. Being deeply present on one platform that sends you well-matched clients is better than being passively listed on five that send you nothing or wrong-fit leads.

What commission is typical for trainer platforms?

Commission ranges from 15% to 40% depending on the platform. 15% to 20% is reasonable. Anything over 30% starts to meaningfully cut into your income, especially at lower price points.

Can I use a platform alongside my own client acquisition?

Yes — and you should. Platforms are one channel, not a complete strategy. But for trainers who hate cold outreach and social media, a good matching platform can replace both entirely.
